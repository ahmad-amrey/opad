OpenFOAM @OF_VERSION@ for OPAD (pack revision @PACK_REV@, Windows x64)
====================================================================

This folder holds programs from OpenFOAM, which OPAD runs as separate programs for its airflow and air-cooling
studies: blockMesh, snappyHexMesh, simpleFoam, splitMeshRegions, topoSet, createBaffles, createPatch,
scalarTransportFoam, buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam and chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam, with the libraries they use.

Source
------
Built without MPI (Pstream's dummy library) by OPAD's recipe, engines/openfoam/make-pack.sh in OPAD's repository,
from the unmodified OpenCFD source tarball OpenFOAM-@OF_VERSION@.tgz (https://dl.openfoam.com/source/@OF_VERSION@/),
with the patches in the recipe's patches/ folder (none when the folder is empty) and zlib-@ZLIB_VERSION@.tar.gz.
The exact source archives and the recipe (opad-openfoam-recipe.tar.gz) are published next to this pack on the
same release: https://github.com/ahmad-amrey/opad/releases (tags engines-openfoam-*).
